# Knight of Champions 2025

Knight of Champions consisted exclusively of fighters who had previously been declared champion.

Knight of Champions 2025 had two winners - champion of the night, and champion of the year. Valkyrie defeated Sourpuss to claim champion of the night, while Sourpuss defeated The Valkyrie in a seperate match to be crowned champion of the year.

The following fighters fought at Knight of Champions 2025:

- Miss Fortune
- The Dark Knight
- Perfect Primrose
- The Green Knight
- Forgemaster
- Sir Tendeth
- The Valkyrie
- Sourpuss
