F(r)ight Knight 2026

F(r)ight Knight 24 was a Halloween-themed Fight Knight. Miss Fortune, marking her first appearance as a fighter since Fight Knight 7, earned the attention of Lady Death. Lady Death defeated Miss Fortune and harvested her soul.

The Coyote continued his exposé of the Bolognese Industrial Complex, and Aphrodite made her first appearance as a fighter. Perfect Primrose, losing a fight against The Coyote, earned the title "Problematic Primrose".

The Highlander was declared champion of Fight Knight 24, earning the title after a fight against The Coyote.

The following fighters fought in Fight Knight 24:

  • Aphrodite
  • Miss Fortune
  • Perfect Primrose
  • Lady Death
  • The Dragon
  • The Coyote
  • The Highlander

Sword Harvest - Fight Knight 23

During Sword Harvest, fighters collected swords from defeated opponents, with the fighter holding the most swords at the end of the night declared champion.

The Coyote publically accused The Dragon and Perfect Primrose of colluding to create a "Bolognese Industrial Complex" that funneled fighters into Fight Knight. Challenging The Dragon, The Coyote fought for the right to have students, rather than instructors, run classes at La Guilde d'Escrime Historique as their hearts see fit (including the right to give each other concussions).

A warden—later revealed to be Aphrodite—was found to be writing erotic fan-fiction staring the fighters.

Silver made their first appearance as a fighter, stealing The Dragon's sword to show their contempt for Italian swordfighting. The Dragon challenged Silver, defeating them and accusing Silver of hypocritically using Italian swordfighting styles when they began losing the fight.

When counting swords at the end of night, Spiral was positioned to take the crown, with a one-sword lead over The Dragon. However, The Coyote, realising that Sourpuss was leading the Bolognese Industrial Complex and colluding to have Spiral declared champion, donated his sword to The Dragon. Now with an equal number of swords, Spiral and The Dragon fought for the role of champion. Ultimately, Spiral defeated The Dragon and took the crown.

The following fighters fought in Fight Knight 23:

  • The Coyote
  • The Dragon
  • Silver
  • Spiral
  • The Golden Hawk
  • The Badger
