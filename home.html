Welcome to the Official Fight Knight Wiki!

Fight Knight - The Modern Gladiator Show is a monthly swordfighting show put on by La Guilde d'Escrime Historique in Montréal, Québec, Canada.

Performers are trained in one or more forms of Historical European Martial Arts (HEMA), displaying historical swordfighting techniques learned at La Guilde d'Escrime Historique. Performers demonstrate their unique sword skills, show off custom costuming, settle rivalries with other fighters, and compete to earn the favour of the spectating crowd.

The winner of each round of Fight Knight is determined by the audience, with volumn from cheering used as a metric of fighter support. The winner of the final round is declared Fight Knight champion for that month, earning a custom-made crown as a token of their victory.

Each show features a raffle for a HEMA-capable training sword provided by Fight Knight's sponsor, Historical Combat Armory. Audience members are also encouraged to heckle fighters, with the night's best heckler winning a free month of classes at La Guilde d'Escrime Historique.

This wiki documents the fighters who perform at Fight Knight and the ongoing lore that develops during each month's show.
